How Meet Bundle keeps you safe

Reviewed photos

Every photo is checked before other members can see it. Photos that break the rules are rejected.

Verified profiles

A blue check shows that a profile is verified.

Adults only

Meet Bundle is for adults 18 and over. Everyone gives a date of birth when they sign up.

Private location

Others see your city and an approximate distance, never your exact location.

Block and report

Every profile and chat has Block and Report, and our team reviews every report.

You control visibility

Choose whether others can see when you were last online.

Tips for meeting someone new

  • Keep the conversation in the app until you feel comfortable.
  • Meet for the first time in a public place.
  • Tell a friend where you’re going and when.
  • Never send money to someone you haven’t met.
  • If something feels wrong, block and report. We’ll take it from there.

Questions about safety

Are photos on Meet Bundle checked?

Yes. Every photo is reviewed before anyone else can see it.

Can people see where I live?

No. Others see your city and an approximate distance, never your exact location or address.

How do I report someone?

Open their profile or your chat with them, tap the menu (⋮) and choose Report.

Who can join Meet Bundle?

Meet Bundle is for adults 18 and over. Everyone gives a date of birth when they sign up.
