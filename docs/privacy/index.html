Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Translations of this page are provided for convenience. If a translation differs from the English version, the English version applies.

Bundle is a dating app run by Meet Bundle (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

InformationExamplesWhy
AccountEmail, password (stored encrypted), sign-in methodTo create and secure your account
ProfileFirst name, birthday, gender, who you want to meet, photos, interests, optional answers such as orientation, relationship status, languages and About meTo show your profile and suggest people
LocationYour city, and your device location if you allow itTo show people near you. Others see your city and an approximate distance, never your exact location.
ActivityLikes, Winks, Sparks, passes, matches and messagesTo run the app’s features
SafetyReports, blocks, photo review resultsTo keep Bundle safe
DeviceDevice type, app version, notification tokenTo send notifications and fix problems

Your birthday is used to show your age; it is never shown on your profile. Some answers, such as orientation, are sensitive. They are always optional, and you can remove them at any time.

How we use it

  • Show your profile to people who match your preferences, and theirs to you.
  • Review photos before others see them, and act on reports.
  • Send notifications you’ve agreed to, such as new Likes, matches and messages.
  • Keep accounts secure and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Understand how the website is used, with cookie-free analytics.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it for third-party advertising.

Who sees what

Other members see your profile: name, age, city, photos, interests and any answers you choose to add. Your messages are visible only to you and the person you’re talking to, except when a message is reported and reviewed for safety.

We share data only with service providers who help us run Bundle, such as hosting, email delivery and photo moderation, under contracts that limit how they use it, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, matches and messages, except safety records kept for up to 12 months and records the law requires.

Delete your account

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or remove profile information at any time in the app.
  • Turn off location and notifications in your phone’s settings.
  • Choose whether others can see when you were last online.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by email: support@meetbundle.com

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or Israel’s Privacy Protection Law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Age

Bundle is for adults 18 and over. We remove accounts we learn belong to anyone younger.

Security

We use encryption in transit, restricted staff access and regular reviews. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we make important changes, we’ll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
