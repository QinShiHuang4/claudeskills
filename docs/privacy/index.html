Meet Bundle is a dating app operated by Or Berkovich, an individual developer based in Israel. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

InformationExamplesWhy
AccountEmail, password (stored as a secure hash), sign-in method, and your Google account ID if you sign in with GoogleTo create and secure your account
ProfileDisplay name, date of birth, gender, dating preferences, photos, interests and optional answersTo show your profile and suggest people
LocationYour city, and your device location if you allow itTo show people near you. Others see your city, never your exact location.
ActivityLikes, Winks, Sparks, passes, matches, messages, online and last-active statusTo run the app’s features

We need your sign-in details and required profile fields to set up your account. We use your date of birth to check eligibility and show your age. Contact support to correct it.

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your verified email address and your Google account ID with us. We use them to create your account and recognise you when you sign in again. We never receive your Google password and don’t keep Google sign-in tokens.

Sexual orientation is optional. Authorized moderators can see it. Members can see it only if you choose to show it on your profile. You can hide or remove it in the app.

You can use a selected city for nearby matching and turn off device location in your phone settings. Turning off permission stops new readings. Choose city-based matching to replace the saved device location.

How we use it

  • Show your profile to people who match your preferences, and theirs to you.
  • Review photos before others see them, and act on reports.
  • Send notifications you’ve agreed to, such as new Likes, matches and messages.
  • Keep accounts secure and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Measure website traffic and performance using Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it for third-party advertising.

Where required by law, we rely on contract necessity to provide core features, legitimate interests for security and abuse prevention where permitted, consent for uses that require it, and legal obligations for required disclosures or retention.

Who sees what

Members can see your profile and the messages you send them. Authorized staff can review profile information, reports, messages included in reports, and excerpts flagged by automated moderation.

Service providers include Render for hosting, AWS for photo storage, Resend for email, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications, Google for sign-in with Google, Sentry for error diagnostics, and Cloudflare for website analytics. We may also disclose data when legally required.

Production hosting and photo storage are in Singapore. Providers may process data in other countries. Contact us for details about international transfers and applicable safeguards.

How long we keep it

When you delete your account, we remove your profile from view, end your matches, and redact the messages you sent. We also delete your preferences, notification settings, Likes, Winks, passes and blocks, and remove the IP address and device information from your sign-in sessions. Limited account, match and message records remain. Photos are removed in the background. A deleted or replaced photo can be recovered from private storage for 7 days and is then permanently removed. Backups expire on their own schedule.

The app temporarily caches viewed photos on the device for up to seven days.

We keep the IP address and browser or app information of a sign-in session for 90 days after the session ends, and then delete them.

Reports and copies of messages included in them can remain after blocking, unmatching or account deletion. We keep them while the case is open and for 90 days after it closes, so decisions can be reviewed. We keep them until our team releases them when a report concerns child safety, when the reported account is suspended or banned, or when the law requires it. We keep other records as required by law.

If an account is closed because its owner is under 18, or is deleted while banned, we keep a one-way coded version of its email address, never the address itself, so it can’t be used to sign up again. We keep it for 90 days after an under-18 closure, and permanently after a ban.

When installation monitoring is enabled, we store a hashed app-installation identifier to help detect abuse. Its account link expires 90 days after it was last seen, or on account deletion.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or remove profile information at any time in the app.
  • Turn off location and notifications in your phone’s settings.
  • Choose whether to share your online and last-active status in the app’s visibility settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by email: support@meetbundle.com

Depending on applicable law, you may also withdraw consent, object to or restrict processing, request a portable copy, or complain to your data protection authority.

Age

Meet Bundle is for adults 18 and over. We remove accounts we learn belong to anyone younger.

Security

We use encryption in transit, restricted staff access and regular reviews. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we make important changes, we’ll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
