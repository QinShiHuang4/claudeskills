Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Translations of this page are provided for convenience. If a translation differs from the English version, the English version applies.

Bundle is a dating app for adults, run by Meet Bundle. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out our standards and how to report a concern.

Our standards

  • Adults only. Bundle is for people aged 18 and over. Everyone must give a date of birth when they join, and anyone under 18 is blocked from creating an account.
  • Zero tolerance. We prohibit any content or behavior that sexualizes, exploits or endangers minors. That includes CSAM, grooming, sexual solicitation of a minor, sextortion, and trafficking.
  • No minors in content. Profile photos and messages must not show or refer to minors in any sexual or exploitative way.
  • No exceptions. These standards apply to every user, every profile, every photo and every message.

How we prevent abuse

  • Photo review. Every photo is reviewed before other members can see it. Photos that break our rules are rejected and the account can be restricted.
  • Age checks. We collect date of birth at sign-up and remove accounts we learn belong to anyone under 18.
  • Reporting and blocking. Every profile and chat has Report and Block options.
  • Human moderation. Our team reviews reports, prioritizing child safety reports above all others.
  • Account restrictions. We can restrict an account at any time while we investigate.

How to report a concern

In the app

  1. Open the person’s profile, or your chat with them.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) and choose Report.
  3. Choose the reason that fits best and add any details.

By email

Email us with the subject “Child safety”: support@meetbundle.com

Include the person’s name as shown on Bundle and what you saw. Please don’t send us images of the abuse. Describe it instead.

If a child is in immediate danger

Contact your local emergency services or police first. In Israel, call 100.

You can also report CSAM to these hotlines:

  • NCMEC CyberTipline
  • INHOPE

How we respond

  • We review child safety reports as our highest priority.
  • We remove violating content and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • We preserve relevant information as required by law to support investigations.
  • We report confirmed CSAM and child exploitation to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement in the relevant country, as the law requires.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests made through proper legal channels.

Compliance

Meet Bundle complies with applicable child safety laws in the countries where Bundle is available, and with Google Play’s and Apple’s child safety requirements.

Child safety contact
