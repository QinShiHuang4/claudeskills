Account

Delete your Bundle account.

This page explains how to delete your account with Bundle, the dating app run by Meet Bundle, and what happens to your data.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Bundle and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Open Account details.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Request deletion here

Can’t open the app, or already uninstalled it? Send a request and we’ll delete your account within 30 days. We’ll email you to confirm it’s really you before we delete anything.

What we delete

  • Your profile, photos, interests and answers
  • Your Likes, Winks, Sparks, matches and chats
  • Your email sign-in and account settings

What we keep, and for how long

  • Reports and safety records about your account, for up to 12 months, to keep people safe and stop banned users from returning.
  • Records we must keep by law, only as long as the law requires.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days.
